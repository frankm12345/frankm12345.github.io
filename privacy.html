Privacy Policy

Health is a personal project used only by its developer. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's consent via Google OAuth, the app reads activity, sleep, heart-rate, health-metric, profile and settings data from the Google Health API. Access is read-only; the app never writes, edits or deletes health data.

How data is used and stored

Data is downloaded to and stored on the developer's own computer for personal analysis. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Locally stored data can be deleted by removing the app's data files.
